Setups compare what the crowd feels with what news and price are doing. Drift: new, material good news that price confirmed and the crowd isn't yet euphoric about. Overreaction dip: a sharp drop on news that isn't bad, bought only after a 1-ATR bounce (or a break of the last session's high). Hype fade: euphoria and a run-up with no confirmed news. Hope trap: falling price and bad news while the crowd stays bullish. A setup decides the call; stocks with no setup use the score (BUY ≥ 65, or ≥ 72 in a weak market, with technical ≥ 55; SELL ≤ 40). Score = social 20% · catalyst 20% · technical 20% · prospects 15% · smart money 15% · attention 10%, minus penalties (euphoria −10, earnings inside the window −5). Stops are 1.5×ATR (or the 10-day low), targets 2R. The crowd read comes from StockTwits posts, whose users lean bullish by default. Experimental research output, not financial advice — judge each setup by its track record before trading on it.
